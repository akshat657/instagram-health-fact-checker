when carbohydrate-rich foods (like wheat flour) are cooked at high temperatures (>120°C), a chemical called acrylamide can form via the Maillard reaction (between asparagine and reducing sugars).

Acrylamide & cancer classification
	•	International Agency for Research on Cancer (IARC) classifies acrylamide as Group 2A — Probable human carcinogen
	•	Classification is based mainly on:
	•	Strong animal data
	•	Mechanistic evidence (genotoxicity after metabolic activation)

Human epidemiological evidence

Large cohort and case-control studies show:
	•	No consistent or strong association between usual dietary acrylamide intake and most human cancers
